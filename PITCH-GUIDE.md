# Shanghai Octopus — website presentation guide

## The 60-second pitch

“People already discover restaurants on their phones. The problem is that when someone hears about Shanghai Octopus, they may have to search through different pages just to see the food, understand the experience, find the address or ask for a table. This website brings all of that into one beautiful place that represents the restaurant properly.

“Look at the first impression: the brand, the food, the atmosphere and the location. Then look at the gallery — guests can swipe through thirty large photographs, tap any one to view it full-screen, and move through the collection like a premium restaurant photo experience.

“Most importantly, the website does more than look good. A guest can choose a reservation date, time and party size, then send the request directly to the restaurant on WhatsApp. The team receives the details in a ready-to-read message and confirms the booking themselves. It also works on mobile, where most customers will be viewing it.

“The idea is simple: make Shanghai Octopus easier to discover, easier to trust and easier to contact — while giving the restaurant a professional online home it controls.”

## Live demo order (about 2 minutes)

1. **Start on the hero.** Show the brand treatment and the immediate food-and-dining impression.
2. **Scroll through the food sections.** Explain that listed dishes and prices should be checked with management before launch; the current design avoids inventing prices.
3. **Spend time in the gallery.** On a phone, swipe naturally. Tap a photograph to open the full-screen viewer, then swipe through using the arrows or keyboard on desktop.
4. **Show the booking preview.** Explain the simple discovery → choose → WhatsApp flow.
5. **Submit a test reservation.** Use a demo name and date, then show the prefilled WhatsApp message. Do not send it as a real booking; the restaurant must confirm availability.
6. **Finish with the map, address and direct contact details.** Ask which official photos, menu, phone/WhatsApp number and social links they want used for the final launch.

## Strong close

“If this were the official Shanghai Octopus website, every person you share the link with would have one place to see the restaurant, explore the food, find the location and send a reservation request. Would you like us to make this the restaurant’s official website and feature your own best dish photographs throughout?”

## Before publishing

- The opening gallery images show the restaurant exterior and dining room. The food imagery is cuisine-led photography selected to create a polished visual presentation; do not describe every dish image as a photograph of a Shanghai Octopus plate.
- Confirm the current menu, dish names, prices, opening hours, address, telephone number and WhatsApp destination with management.
- Test the WhatsApp destination on an actual phone and ensure the restaurant controls that number.
- Confirm the map pin and all business details with management.
- Add the restaurant's official Instagram/TikTok links and any approved brand assets.
- Use a domain owned by the restaurant and deploy over HTTPS.

## What the booking form does

It prepares a WhatsApp message containing the guest's name, request type, preferred date/time, party size and notes. It does **not** reserve a table automatically, take payment, or claim that a reservation is confirmed. The restaurant confirms requests directly.
